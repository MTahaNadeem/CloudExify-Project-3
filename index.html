About Us

Caring for Smiles Since 2008

SmileCare Dental Clinic was established in 2008 with a single mission: to deliver world-class dental care to the people of Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Over 15 years, we have grown into a 12-specialist clinic trusted by over 500 families across the twin cities. We combine cutting-edge dental technology with a warm, patient-first approach — because we believe great dental care should feel as comfortable as it is effective.
