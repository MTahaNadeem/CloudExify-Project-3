Professional in-clinic whitening at OraDent is completely safe. We use medically approved gels and UV light technology. You may experience mild sensitivity for 24-48 hours post-treatment, which is completely normal and temporary. We recommend using sensitivity toothpaste the day before your appointment.

At OraDent we perform most root canals in a single session of 60-90 minutes using rotary endodontics. Complex cases with multiple canals may require a second visit. The procedure is done under local anesthesia — most patients report feeling only pressure, not pain. You can return to normal activities the same day.

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The titanium post fuses to your jawbone (osseointegration) making it as stable as a natural tooth root. The crown on top typically lasts 15-25 years. We provide complete aftercare instructions and annual follow-ups are included in the first year.

We recommend a child's first dental visit by age 1 or within 6 months of their first tooth appearing. Early visits help identify developmental issues, build comfort with the dentist, and establish good oral hygiene habits. We have a dedicated gentle approach for pediatric patients at OraDent.

Treatment duration depends on the complexity of the case. Mild crowding may be corrected in 6-9 months. Moderate cases typically take 12-18 months. Complex bite corrections can take up to 24 months. Clear aligners generally take 6-18 months. We create a precise timeline at your initial consultation.

Whitening removes stains from your natural tooth enamel — ideal for discolouration from tea, coffee, or smoking. Veneers are thin porcelain shells bonded to the front of teeth — they correct shape, size, and colour simultaneously, and are permanent. We recommend whitening for staining only; veneers for chipped, uneven, or heavily discoloured teeth.

We recommend a routine checkup and cleaning every 6 months for most patients. Patients with ongoing orthodontic treatment, gum disease, or diabetes may need quarterly visits. Children benefit from 3-monthly checkups during active growth years. Regular visits prevent 90% of major dental issues before they become costly problems.
